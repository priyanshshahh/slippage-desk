Slippage Desk

The options agent that measures its own execution.
The problem, as arithmetic

A credit spread is a thin trade by construction.

A 0.24-delta spread collects$81.09
A win banks 40% of it+$32.43
The stop costs all of it-$81.09
So breakeven is71.4%
A 0.24 delta implies76% OTM
The entire edge is 4.6 percentage points. $5.19 a contract.
Where that edge goes
Crossing the bid/ask cost $1.78 a contract.
34% of the entire edge.
Execution is not a rounding error here. It is a first-order term, and it is the one thing four sessions can actually measure.
I read all 47 other submissions
Four mention slippage. All four are pre-trade filters.
Not one measures what it got after the fill.
Eleven promise their model can only veto. Every one of them measures whether its decisions were right. None measures whether its executions were good.
The live deskEvery figure on this page is derived from the config and from the contracts actually filled. Nothing is typed by hand.
Live evidencePer-bucket capture: underlying, tenor, delta band, time of day. Buckets that fill readily hold out for mid. Buckets that do not, pay up or get skipped.
