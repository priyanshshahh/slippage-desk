Slippage Desk

The options agent that measures its own execution.
The problem, as arithmetic

A credit spread is a thin trade by construction.

A 0.24-delta spread collects$78.74
A win banks 40% of it+$31.50
The stop costs all of it-$78.74
So breakeven is71.4%
A 0.24 delta implies76% OTM
The entire edge is 4.6 percentage points. $5.04 a contract.
Where that edge goes
Crossing the bid/ask cost $1.38 a contract.
27% of the entire edge.
Execution is not a rounding error here. It is a first-order term, and it is the one thing four sessions can actually measure.

What this does not claim
Four sessions cannot establish that a strategy is profitable.
Not from a window this short.
What four sessions can establish is that an agent measured its own execution and acted on it. That is the claim, and the file above is the receipt.
